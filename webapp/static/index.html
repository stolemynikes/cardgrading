Front and back flat, card filling the frame, margin around it, matte backing. For surface relief, add 3–6 shots per side: keep the camera and the light where they are and turn the card 90° each time. Scanning instead? 1200 dpi, TIFF or PNG, and turn off auto-crop, auto-colour, unsharp mask, descreen and dust removal.
Capture protocol: dark matte background · phone on a tripod, directly overhead · diffuse light from two sides · if the image goes blurry you're inside the lens's focus range — back away ~20cm and use the zoom slider instead.
